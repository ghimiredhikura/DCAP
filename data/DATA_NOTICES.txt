DCAP processed Nepal landslide dataset — Round 1 release, 10 November 2026

This separate dataset contains 1,320 losslessly compressed image/mask pairs
for 2017–2019. Raster values are unchanged from the study. Images contain
14 stored channels; the code derives six reflectance-change channels and
validity. Masks use 0 (provisional background), 1 (landslide), 255 (ignored).
These are modified research products, not official provider products.
